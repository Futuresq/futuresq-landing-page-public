Last updated: May 9, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the website futuresq.com operated by Futuresq UG (haftungsbeschränkt). It explains what personal data we process when you visit this site and on what legal basis. Separate privacy notices apply to our products (e.g. Sparly).

3. Hosting and server logs

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service provided by GitHub Inc. (USA). On each request, GitHub processes standard server-log data (IP address, timestamp, requested URL and HTTP status, referrer, user agent) for security and stability.

Legal basis: Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR — our legitimate interest in stable and secure operation. GitHub acts as a processor (Art. 28 GDPR); data may be transferred to the United States, safeguarded by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and GitHub's certification under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework. Details: GitHub Privacy Statement.

4. Contact by email

If you contact us by email (e.g. via the mailto: links on this site), the data you provide (email address, message content, any further details you choose to include) will be processed by us solely for the purpose of handling your request. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (b) GDPR (pre-contractual or contractual measures) or Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR (legitimate interest in answering inquiries). Messages are stored for as long as necessary to handle the matter and to comply with statutory retention obligations.

5. Cookies and local storage

This website does not set tracking cookies. We use a single technical entry in your browser's localStorage (key: futuresq-lang) to remember your selected display language. This entry contains only the value en or de, is stored only on your device, and is never transmitted to us. The legal basis is Art. 6 (1) (f) GDPR — providing the site in your preferred language. You can clear this entry at any time via your browser's site-data settings.

6. Analytics, tracking, and third-party services

We do not use Google Analytics, Matomo, Plausible, or any other analytics service on this website. We do not embed third-party widgets, fonts, maps, video players, or social-media plugins. No advertising or remarketing pixels are loaded.

7. External links

This website contains links to external websites operated by third parties (e.g. GitHub, the European Commission ODR platform, the LfDI BW website). We have no influence over the content or the data processing practices of these external sites. Please consult their respective privacy policies before submitting any information.

8. Security

All data exchanged between your browser and this website is encrypted in transit using TLS, provided automatically by our hosting provider. We do not operate any of our own servers for this website and therefore do not store user data ourselves; the protective measures applied to server-log data lie with GitHub. We will continually adapt our security measures in line with technological progress.

9. Your rights

  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)

  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)
  • Right to object (Art. 21 GDPR)
  • Right to withdraw consent at any time, where processing is based on consent (Art. 7 (3) GDPR)

To exercise these rights, please contact us at contact@futuresq.com.
